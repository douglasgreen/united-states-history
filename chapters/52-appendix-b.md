# Appendix B. Tables and Data

*Stage 2 seed — October 29, 2026. Page allowance: 10 pages. Appendices. Source: [README](../README.md), Appendix B. This is a seed copied from the book plan, not a researched outline or a draft.*

## Outline from README

**Purpose:** Supply eight compact, explicitly defined reference tables. Each may have labeled panels, but a panel is not a new table number. The table register below defines the precise scope and candidate series. These remain `planned`; the draft is an inventory, not the data themselves.

1. Population and urbanization, 1630–2020: bounded colonial estimates, census benchmarks, regional/city comparisons, and stated geographic changes.
2. Presidential elections, 1789–2024: candidates, party, electoral votes, reported popular vote and turnout where comparable; no invented national popular vote before meaningful coverage exists.
3. Territorial growth and major treaties, 1763–1917: acquisitions, cessions, sovereignty/control distinctions, instrument dates, area where defensible, and nominal cost with units.
4. Wars and military mobilization: selected conflicts through 2021, service totals, mortality definitions/estimates, and comparable cost measures only where sourced; distinguish appropriations from expenditure and long-term costs.
5. Immigration, 1820–2020, with separately labeled colonial context: admissions by decade/origin and the 1921, 1924, and 1929 quota systems; do not equate admissions, net migration, foreign-born stock, and unauthorized population.
6. Slavery and coerced movement: selected 17th-century Caribbean benchmarks, the 1790–1860 enslaved census population, and independently sourced Atlantic/domestic-trade estimates and price benchmarks; never treat the transatlantic database as a domestic-trade census.
7. Economic and communications benchmarks, 1790–2024 where series permit: public finance, railroad mileage, steel, unemployment, prices, output per capita, inequality, union density, selected labor-force measures, newspapers/telephones, and regional employment. Label panels and breaks; do not imply all measures share one uninterrupted series.
8. Land use, public works, and settlement, 1930–1940: TVA/CCC locations and counts, drought/land-use context, and Dust Bowl migration benchmarks needed for Map 26. Remove the old unresearched promise of continental bison, forest, carbon-dioxide, and extreme-weather series; those topics remain in prose where supported, not as nonexistent appendix datasets.

The chapter-table crosswalk is maintained here in the README, not as an extra published ninth data table. Correct bad references by matching the subject: population to Table 1, elections to Table 2, cessions to Table 3, wars to Table 4, immigration to Table 5, slavery to Table 6. Legal instruments and public-opinion surveys also require direct sources; not every chapter comparison must be forced into Appendix B.
